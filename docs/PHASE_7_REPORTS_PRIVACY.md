# Phase 7 — Reports + Privacy

Implemented in source on 2026-09-23 without changing the frozen Build 0.17 requirements.

- Scope-aware report endpoints for Regional Leader, Itorero, Igihande and Itsinda accounts.
- Believer participation report uses **Yego/Oya**.
- Individual contribution amounts are hidden from ordinary church/section/group report users.
- Regional Leader may view contribution amounts under the approved privacy exception.
- Budget performance returns Target, Achievement, Percentage and Remaining; progress may exceed 100% and Remaining stops at zero.
- Date range and contribution-type filters are supported for contribution reporting.

Status: IMPLEMENTED IN SOURCE. Not yet Windows-tested, hosted-tested, or release-certified.
